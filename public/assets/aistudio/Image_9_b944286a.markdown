## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, precision-driven CRM interface designed for ambitious sales teams, revenue leaders, and modern relationship managers. The visual identity sits at the intersection of deep-sea atmospheric minimalism and high-voltage technical precision.

- **Brand Personality**: Insightful, decisive, razor-sharp, and motivating. It avoids bureaucratic enterprise clutter in favor of an energetic command-center feel.
- **Target Audience**: High-growth sales representatives, account executives, and revenue operations directors who live in metrics, pipeline velocity, and client communications daily.
- **Emotional Response**: Empowering control, clarity amid high data density, and an immediate rush of momentum upon converting leads or hitting targets.
- **Design Movement**: Contemporary Dark-Mode Precision Glassmorphism. The system employs deeply saturated oceanic canvases, refined translucent surface sheets, low-contrast luminous ghost borders, and surgical bursts of high-vis acid lime to draw focus instantly to what drives revenue.

## Colors

The palette leverages a dark teal baseline paired with high-impact chartreuse/lime accents to achieve clinical focus and contrast.

- **Primary (`#D3EC00`)**: Electric Lime / Acid Green. Reserved strictly for forward-moving actions, critical deal stages, active metrics, primary conversion buttons, and high-priority pipeline alerts. Text on primary fills is fixed to `#051C24` for peak contrast.
- **Secondary (`#00E5FF`)**: Cyan Neon. Used for secondary telemetry, communication pulses (inbound emails, calls), and secondary data visualizations.
- **Tertiary (`#1E4E5F`)**: Mid-tier Petrol. Serves as active container highlights, subtle interactive states, and chip backdrops.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#051C24`)**: Deep Oceanic Petrol. The master canvas backdrop that grounds the entire CRM experience without producing pure-black eye strain.
- **Tonal Layers & Translucencies**:
  - `Surface Sub-level`: `#04151B`
  - `Surface Card Base`: `rgba(10, 39, 50, 0.72)` (Hex reference: `#0A2732`)
  - `Surface Card Raised`: `rgba(14, 52, 66, 0.85)` (Hex reference: `#0E3442`)
  - `Surface Border / Rim`: `rgba(211, 236, 0, 0.12)` on focus/hover; default `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - `Text Primary`: `#F0FDF4`
  - `Text Muted`: `#7E9CA7`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the sleek, humanized geometric curves of **Outfit** for structural UI framing and conversational executive prose with the rigorous technical precision of **Space Mono** for telemetry, currency, timestamps, and pipeline stages.

- **Editorial Headings (`Outfit`)**: Fluid, confident, and warm. Gives deal names, account headers, and greeting titles an approachable yet polished executive polish.
- **Body Text (`Outfit`)**: Ensures extended communications, client conversation threads, and deal notes remain effortlessly legible and friendly.
- **Numeric & Data Typography (`Space Mono`)**: All monetary figures (`$124,500`), lead scores (`98/100`), contract IDs, and table indices utilize `Space Mono`. Monospaced tabular alignment guarantees vertical scanning integrity across deals and revenue forecasts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a flexible 12-column adaptive grid designed to accommodate wide multi-pipeline boards, dense data grids, and collapsible communication drawers.

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with `gutter-desktop` (24px) and outer canvas margins of `margin-desktop` (40px). Supports persistent collapsed left navigation (72px or 240px expanded) and dynamic right-side contact panels.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with `gutter` (20px) and outer margin of `margin-tablet` (24px). Pipeline columns scroll horizontally along a snap-track.
  - **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4 columns with `space-md` gutters and `margin` (16px). Pipeline stages switch to segmented tab views.
- **Rhythm Principle**: Internal card padding standardizes on `space-lg` (24px) for dashboard summary widgets and `space-md` (16px) for pipeline deal tiles to maximize tactical density without crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent planar layering, backdrop filtering, and ambient cyan-tinted atmospheric glows rather than opaque dropshadows.

- **Canvas Foundation**: Base background layer sits at `#051C24`.
- **Card Level 1 (Resting Sheets)**: Background set to `rgba(10, 39, 50, 0.70)` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border is a crisp 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`. No heavy dropshadows.
- **Card Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Modals)**: Background elevates to `rgba(14, 52, 66, 0.90)` backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border transitions to `rgba(211, 236, 0, 0.25)`. A diffused ambient edge-glow is applied: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(211, 236, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Drawers**: Solid-backed petrol `#07212B` with a left/top border highlighting at `rgba(211, 236, 0, 0.20)` and an ambient deep drop shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(2, 10, 14, 0.70)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, highly modern geometry that balances technical structure with high tactile comfort.

- **Containers & Surface Cards**: Default to `rounded-lg` (16px) and large dashboard panels to `rounded-xl` (24px). This softens the high-contrast data presentation.
- **Pills & Indicator Badges**: Deal statuses, tags, KPI flags, and micro-metrics are strictly fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create clear geometric contrast against rectangular data surfaces.
- **Buttons & Action Controls**: Inputs and primary actions adopt an 8px to 12px radius, preserving an intentional, clickable silhouette.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Electric Lime (`#D3EC00`) fill with `#051C24` bold typography (`Outfit` 600). Hover state features subtle brightness escalation (`#E2FD0D`) accompanied by a neon glow aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 18px rgba(211, 236, 0, 0.35)`). Active state applies a subtle scale compress (`0.98`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Translucent background (`rgba(14, 52, 66, 0.5)`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F0FDF4`. Hover triggers border transition to `rgba(211, 236, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Destructive**: Translucent dark crimson tint (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`) with crimson border and `#FCA5A5` text.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). 
- Status chips leverage low-opacity tinted grounds (`rgba(211, 236, 0, 0.1)`) paired with uppercase mono text (`Space Mono`, 11px, weight 700) in `#D3EC00`.
- Stale or idle deal indicators utilize muted teal (`rgba(126, 156, 167, 0.15)`) with `#7E9CA7` typography.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `rgba(10, 39, 50, 0.60)` with inset ghost border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Radius `10px`. 
- Focus state activates an instantaneous crisp lime border highlight `rgba(211, 236, 0, 0.8)` with a faint outer ring glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(211, 236, 0, 0.15)`. Text inputs utilize `Outfit`, while currency and numerical inputs immediately switch to `Space Mono`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkbox squares utilize an 6px radius; radios are circular.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` over clear background.
- Checked: `#D3EC00` background fill with a deep navy `#051C24` check mark icon, instantly recognizable against the dark canvas.

### Cards & Pipeline Tiles
- Built with translucent glass backing (`#0A2732` at 75% opacity with 12px blur).
- Draggable pipeline cards feature a delicate left indicator strip colored by deal probability or urgency.
- Dragging state increases elevation, elevates card opacity to 95%, and casts an accent glow.

### Deal Metric Widget (Domain-Specific)
- Prominent KPI widgets house large `data-metric` figures in `Space Mono` alongside an inline delta indicator (e.g., `▲ +18.4%` in `#D3EC00`). 
- Features micro-sparklines rendered in `#00E5FF` layered over a faint glowing gradient fill.
